Game modes

Three ways to read the signal

Every mode runs the same honest contract — twenty-five trials, five choices, one impression at a time. What changes is how the signal reaches you.

MindSight

Learn to see with your intuition

MindSight is a training practice built on an unusual premise: that perception doesn't have to travel through the eyes. Practitioners work blindfolded until what they call Windows begin to open — stretches of full sight with the eyes still covered. It's a skill that's been getting a lot more attention lately.

The hard part is practising alone

Traditionally this training takes two people. One holds up a card; the other sits blindfolded and calls it; the first one says whether it was right. No partner, no practice — which is why most people who want to try it never really get started.

Psychic Tournament takes the second person's job. It chooses the target, waits for your call, and tells you what it actually was — through sound and haptics, so none of it needs your eyes. Blindfold on, phone in hand, practise as long as you like.

Recommended path

Freeplay

Unscored, unhurried, and the best place to start. You call the target out loud to yourself, tap, and the app tells you what it was.

  1. Put the blindfold on and open a Freeplay drill.
  2. Let an impression form, and make your guess.
  3. Tap anywhere on the screen.
  4. The app announces the answer, so you know straight away whether your MindSight was accurate.
  • Colours — six, never twice in a row
  • Shapes — the five Zener symbols
  • Words — a random three-letter word

Scored

Same blindfold, same drill — but now you say it out loud and the app keeps the book. For when you want to know whether you're actually improving.

  1. Press and hold anywhere on the screen.
  2. Speak what your MindSight gives you.
  3. Release, and get an immediate hit or miss.
  4. Twenty-five attempts are scored and saved, so your progress is there to look back on.

Hits and misses come back as sound and vibration as well as on screen — one pulse or two, counted out — so you can keep the blindfold on right through a scored session and still know exactly how you're doing.

Built like someone cared

An interface with weather

Drifting nebulae, hundreds of individually twinkling stars, sparkles, the occasional shooting star, and a slow shimmer across the whole surface. Nothing on screen is ever quite still.

Reveals worth waiting for

A perspective card flip, a hit burst in gold, a miss in deep red, and a screen shake that scales with your streak. The feedback is the reward.

Session coordinates

The honest bit

We show you
the chance baseline

Five choices means one in five. Over twenty-five trials, pure guessing averages five hits — and lands anywhere from two to eight without anything unusual happening at all.

So we draw that line on your results and leave it there. Psychic Tournament is a training and entertainment app, not a scientific instrument, and a good score is not evidence of anything paranormal. It's a good score. Enjoy it, then play again.
