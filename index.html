The games

Four ways to read the signal

Every game plays by the same rules: twenty-five calls, five equally likely answers, one impression at a time. What changes is how the signal reaches you.

Symbol intuition

Zener

The classic ESP card test. Read the symbol before the card turns face up.

  • Circle, cross, waves, square or star, each one drawn fresh every turn. There's no deck, so there's nothing to count.
  • Every reveal is a real card flip: gold for a hit, and the true card named when you miss.
  • Streaks climb from Focused to Third Eye, True Sight and beyond, and the sky lights up with them.
  • Tilt your phone and the sky slides away in layers behind the card.
  • Each session gets its own coordinate code you can share.

Hidden signals

Dowsing

Follow the pull and uncover what's buried beneath the field.

  • Five covered positions on a live sonar field: NW, NE, centre, SW and SE.
  • Tap where you feel it. The covers slide away and the target answers.
  • A hit cracks the board open with gold lightning.
  • Its own leaderboard, history and statistics.
25 calls · 5 positions

Precognition

Star Seed

Name the gate before the seed finds it. The outcome doesn't exist yet.

  • Call one of five gates. The same tap releases the seed.
  • It bounces through a fresh constellation every turn: prisms deflect, pulsars throw, vortices pull, and every star shatters on its second hit.
  • Every gate is equally likely. Your call never touches the physics.
  • Miss, and the gate you called stays lit in rose so you can see both halves.

The app tracks conviction calls on their own, because the interesting question isn't whether you beat chance. It's whether you know when you do.

Mindsight

Learn to see with your intuition

Mindsight is blindfold training built on an unusual premise: that perception doesn't have to travel through the eyes. Practitioners work blindfolded until what they call Windows begin to open, stretches of clear sight with the eyes still covered.

The hard part is practising alone

This training usually takes two people. One holds up a card, the other sits blindfolded and calls it, and the first says whether it was right. No partner, no practice, which is why most people who want to try it never really start.

Psychic Tournament does the partner's job. Open Mindsight from the side menu and pick an exercise. The app chooses the target, waits for your call, and tells you what it was with a voice and a count of pulses, so none of it needs your eyes. The screen stays awake while you focus.

Freeplay

Unscored and unhurried. Say your call to yourself, tap, and the app tells you what it was.

  1. Put the blindfold on and open an exercise.
  2. Let an impression form, and make your call.
  3. Tap anywhere on the screen.
  4. The answer is spoken and pulsed, so you know straight away whether you saw it. Colored Shapes says both halves, like "red triangle".

Scored

Same blindfold, same exercise. This time you say your call out loud and the app keeps score. Use it when you want to know whether you're really improving.

  1. Start a scored session. There's nothing to hold down: the app is listening.
  2. Say what your Mindsight gives you: "white", "purple", "blue star".
  3. The round settles the moment you're heard, with an immediate hit or miss.
  4. Twenty-five rounds are scored against that exercise's own chance line and saved to My Performance.

Hits and misses come back as sound and vibration as well as on screen, so you can keep the blindfold on for a whole scored session. Mindsight scores stay on your device and off the leaderboards, because nobody can check whose eyes were covered.

Focus Training · New in 1.1

Practise between sessions

A third mode, next to the Psychic Games and Mindsight. Open the side menu and choose Focus Training to start a guided Mindsight Meditation.

Mindsight Meditation

A calm voice helps you settle, breathe and relax. Then the screen fills with colour, and you're asked what each one feels, sounds and looks like on the inside, sometimes before you're told the colour, sometimes after. At the end, the voice walks you back through the colours you explored.

Underneath it all plays a brainwave entrainment tone: binaural beats, isochronic tones, or a blend of the two. Nothing is scored and nothing is saved. It's practice, not a test.

The honest bit

We show you
the chance baseline

Five choices means one in five. Over twenty-five calls, pure guessing averages five hits, and anywhere from two to eight is perfectly ordinary.

The games are built so that line is true. Zener draws every card independently, so there's no deck to count. Star Seed decides where the seed will land before you call, and your call never touches the physics. Lodestar's comet starts at the exact centre of a perfect circle, and nothing you tap changes where it goes. Mindsight shows each exercise against its own line: one in two for Mono, one in six for Color. Psychic Tournament is a training and entertainment app, not a scientific instrument, and a good score isn't proof of anything paranormal. It's a good score. Enjoy it, then play again.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Is Psychic Tournament free?

Yes. It's free to download on Google Play and the App Store. The app is supported by ads shown between sessions, never during a call or a meditation.

How does the ESP test work?

Each session is twenty-five calls. Every call has five equally likely answers, so guessing alone averages five hits, or 20%. Your result is always shown against that line, so you can see whether you're above it, at it, or below it.

What are Zener cards?

The five-symbol set (circle, cross, waves, square and star) designed by psychologist Karl Zener for J. B. Rhine's ESP experiments at Duke University in the 1930s. Psychic Tournament's Zener game draws each card independently, so there's no deck to count.

What's a good score?

Chance averages 5 out of 25, and anything from 2 to 8 happens routinely by luck. Score 11 or better and you're on the global leaderboard. By chance alone, that happens about once in 180 sessions.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The games, Mindsight training, Focus Training meditations, history and statistics all work with no connection and no account. Leaderboards, Daily Vision and player profiles need a connection, and Daily Vision and profiles need you to sign in.

What is Lodestar?

The psychokinesis game, added in 1.1. You name one of five arcs around a circular field, then hold it in mind while a comet sets off from the centre and wanders until it strikes the ring. The field is a perfect circle and your call never changes the comet's flight, so each arc is one in five. Lodestar is ranked like the other games, with its own leaderboard, XP and Daily Vision.

What is Mindsight training?

Blindfolded perception practice that normally needs a partner to hold up targets. The app takes the partner's place. There are three exercises, Mono, Color and Colored Shapes, each with a Freeplay mode that gives you the answer through sound and vibration, and a Scored mode where you say your call out loud.

What is Focus Training?

Guided Mindsight Meditation in 5, 15 or 25 minutes. A calm voice helps you settle and breathe, then the screen fills with colour and you're asked what each one feels, sounds and looks like on the inside, over a brainwave entrainment tone. Headphones are recommended, because binaural beats need a separate sound in each ear. Without them, sessions use isochronic tones through the speaker.

Is multiplayer coming?

Yes. Realtime telepathy multiplayer is in development: one player sees the outcome and sends it, the other receives it and calls it, and both are rewarded when the message gets through.

Does a high score prove I'm psychic?

No. Psychic Tournament is a training and entertainment app, not a scientific instrument. A good score is a good score. The only result worth paying attention to is one that stays above chance across many sessions.
