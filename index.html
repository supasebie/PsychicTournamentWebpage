Three ways to read the signal

MindSight

Learn to see with your intuition

MindSight is blindfold training built on an unusual premise: that perception doesn't have to travel through the eyes. Practitioners work blindfolded until what they call Windows begin to open, stretches of clear sight with the eyes still covered.

The hard part is practising alone

This training usually takes two people. One holds up a card, the other sits blindfolded and calls it, and the first says whether it was right. No partner, no practice, which is why most people who want to try it never really start.

Psychic Tournament does the partner's job. It picks the target, waits for your call, and tells you what it was through sound and vibration, so none of it needs your eyes. The screen stays awake while you focus. Put the blindfold on and practise as long as you like.

Freeplay

Unscored and unhurried. Say your call to yourself, tap, and the app tells you what it was.

  1. Put the blindfold on and open a drill.
  2. Let an impression form, and make your call.
  3. Tap anywhere on the screen.
  4. The answer plays as a sound and a count of pulses, so you know straight away whether you saw it.
  • Colours · red, green, blue, yellow and purple, one to five pulses
  • Shapes · the five Zener symbols
  • Reading · a random three-letter word

Scored Colour

Same blindfold, same drill. This time you say the colour out loud and the app keeps score. Use it when you want to know whether you're really improving.

  1. Press and hold anywhere on the screen.
  2. Speak the colour your MindSight gives you.
  3. Release, and get an immediate hit or miss.
  4. Twenty-five calls are scored and saved to your history, with the same 20% baseline as every other game.

Hits and misses come back as sound and vibration as well as on screen, so you can keep the blindfold on for a whole scored session. MindSight scores stay on your device and off the leaderboards, because nobody can check whose eyes were covered.

The honest bit

We show you
the chance baseline

Five choices means one in five. Over twenty-five calls, pure guessing averages five hits, and anywhere from two to eight is perfectly ordinary.

The games are built so that line is true. Zener draws every card independently, so there's no deck to count. Star Seed decides where the seed will land before you call, and your call never touches the physics. Psychic Tournament is a training and entertainment app, not a scientific instrument, and a good score isn't proof of anything paranormal. It's a good score. Enjoy it, then play again.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Is Psychic Tournament free?

Yes. It's free to download on Google Play and the App Store. The app is supported by ads shown between sessions, never during a call.

How does the ESP test work?

Each session is twenty-five calls. Every call has five equally likely answers, so guessing alone averages five hits, or 20%. Your result is always shown against that line, so you can see whether you're above it, at it, or below it.

What are Zener cards?

The five-symbol set (circle, cross, waves, square and star) designed by psychologist Karl Zener for J. B. Rhine's ESP experiments at Duke University in the 1930s. Psychic Tournament's Zener game draws each card independently, so there's no deck to count.

What's a good score?

Chance averages 5 out of 25, and anything from 2 to 8 happens routinely by luck. Score 11 or better and you're on the global leaderboard. By chance alone, that happens about once in 180 sessions.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The games, MindSight training, history and statistics all work with no connection and no account. Leaderboards, Daily Vision and player profiles need a connection, and Daily Vision and profiles need you to sign in.

What is MindSight training?

Blindfolded perception practice that normally needs a partner to hold up targets. The app takes the partner's place: it picks a colour, shape or word and gives you the answer through sound and vibration, so you can practise alone with the blindfold on.

Is multiplayer coming?

Yes. Realtime telepathy multiplayer is in development: one player sees the outcome and sends it, the other receives it and calls it, and both are rewarded when the message gets through. A psychokinesis game called Mind Move is on the way too.

Does a high score prove I'm psychic?

No. Psychic Tournament is a training and entertainment app, not a scientific instrument. A good score is a good score. The only result worth paying attention to is one that stays above chance across many sessions.
