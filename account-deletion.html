Delete your account

Psychic Tournament · developer: Joseph Sebastian Ruiz aka SupaSebie

Delete it yourself, in the app

The fastest route, and the one that completes immediately:

  1. Open Psychic Tournament.
  2. Go to Options → Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account at the bottom of your account card.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete forever.

You must be signed in. The account and its data are removed as soon as you confirm; there is no waiting period and no way to undo it.

Request deletion by email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email overninethousanddevelopment@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, with the subject Account deletion request.

Requests are actioned within 30 days. We may need to confirm you control the address before deleting, because the address is the only thing that identifies the account.

What is deleted

  • Your account and its Google sign-in link
  • Your profile, including your leaderboard username and display name
  • Your leaderboard scores and streaks, and your Daily Vision entries
  • Your XP total, level and play streak
  • Registered push notification devices and any queued notifications
  • Your session history stored on the device you delete from

All of it is deleted permanently and at once. Nothing is retained in a recoverable form, and none of it is kept under a placeholder or anonymised profile.

What is not deleted, and why

  • Session history on other devices you have played on. It is stored only on each device and is never uploaded. Uninstalling the app on that device removes it.
  • Guest progress. XP earned before you ever signed in belongs to the device, not to the account, and is left alone. Uninstalling the app removes it.
  • Store subscriptions. An ad-free membership is billed by Google Play or the App Store. Deleting your account does not cancel it — cancel it in your store account first.
  • Aggregated, non-identifying analytics. Counts such as how many sessions were played in a mode contain no identifier that can be traced back to you and are not retained per account. Identified analytics for your account stop at deletion.
